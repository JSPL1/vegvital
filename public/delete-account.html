Delete your VegVital account

VegVital is a vegetarian health tracker by Sushanta Kumar Dash. You can delete your account and all its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open VegVital and tap your avatar (top right) to open Settings.
  2. In Account, tap Delete my account and data.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

On this page

What is deleted

Your account (name, email, password), your profile, all logged meals, water, steps, sleep, workouts and weight history, and all food photos are permanently deleted from our server straight away. Nothing is kept afterwards. Data still on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

Can't log in? Ask for deletion by email using the contact address on VegVital's Google Play page; we delete the account within 30 days.
