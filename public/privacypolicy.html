VegVital Privacy Policy

VegVital is a personal health tracker. It has no account and no server of its own.

Health data

With your permission the app reads steps, sleep, calories burned, heart rate and weight from Health Connect to fill your daily dashboard automatically. This data is stored only on your phone and is never sold or shared.

Food photos

When you take a food photo, the image is sent to Anthropic's Claude API using the API key you entered, solely to estimate the food's nutrition. Photos are not stored by the app after analysis.

Your control

You can revoke Health Connect access at any time in Android settings, and clear all app data from Settings → Data.
